# Distribution campaign — extracted from screenshot

*Source: Slack `# 💲 |campaign-info` channel. The post is from Atomik (APP), dated 11/08/2026 6:33 PM.*

## Campaign details

**Client:** Distribution, a podcast about cutting through the noise, where founders and operators break down the distribution, creator, paid and organic plays behind their GTM and branding.

**Hosts:** Arthur and Subah, plus a different founder/operator guest each episode.

**Platforms:** YouTube, Instagram, X (Twitter) only. TikTok is **not** part of this campaign.

> The best product doesn't win. The best-distributed one does.

Check the current progress of the campaign on the platform.

## What your clips are about

Clips must focus on **Arthur and Subah** and their episodes. Keep the hosts on screen for at least **5–7 seconds** in every clip.

## Minimum views

You can submit a clip as soon as you upload it. Each clip must cross **1,500 views** to be eligible. Once your eligible clips together reach **20,000 views**, you get paid out.

## Post every week

Post at least **1 clip every week** (Monday to Sunday). Miss a week and you get **suspended**. If you're suspended before hitting 20,000 eligible views, you get **no payout**, so stay active.

## Platform balance

Spread your accepted clips across YouTube, Instagram and X:

- **70% or less** on one platform: no penalty.
- **70–90%** on one platform: payout cut by **30%**.
- **More than 90%** on one platform: payout cut by **45%**.

Example with 10 accepted clips: 7 or fewer on one platform is fine, 8–9 is a 30% cut, all 10 is a 45% cut.

## US audience CPM boost

Accounts with **30% or more US audience** get a boosted **$1.25 CPM**, based on your approved account demographics.

## Main rules

- ✅ Spell **Arthur** and **Subah** exactly like that.
- ✅ Correct spelling and grammar everywhere.
- ✅ Clean, single-color text; no flashy or neon colors.
- ✅ You **CAN** post the same clip on different platforms.
- ❌ No profanity, sexual, or explicit content, even censored.
- ❌ No competitor names, comparisons, or bashing.
- ❌ No misleading claims, or claims about the show, its performance, or its viewers that aren't said in the footage.
- ❌ No phonk music.
- ❌ No copying other clippers' work.
- ❌ Don't post the same clip twice on the same platform, even from different accounts.
- ❌ No excessive jump cuts, meme spam, or distracting effects.

## Content moderation

Some source footage may contain things we don't allow in clips. Do **NOT** include:

- Profanity or swearing
- Sexually suggestive or explicit material
- Controversies, scandals, or lawsuits
- Politically sensitive, polarizing, or activist content
- Anything showing a person, group, brand, or company in a negative way
- Anything that could trigger harassment or backlash

Breaking any of these rules can get your clip **rejected** and, for repeat or serious violations, get you **blacklisted from this and future campaigns and removed from the server**.

## Nametags

Spell the hosts' names exactly as **Arthur** and **Subah**. For guests, use the exact spelling from the episode title or the approved footage. Never guess or invent full names or surnames. Not sure? Ask the team in `#🎟️ |open-a-ticket` before posting. Misspellings can get the clip rejected.

## Your account

- Pick a username in the **business / tech / startups / growth** niche. No profanity or suggestive names.
- It must **not** look official. Don't use names like `distribution.clips`, `distribution.unofficial`, `arthur.clips`, or `subah.clips`.
- Use a generic profile photo that fits your page (a well-known founder, a symbol of business and growth, etc.).
- Your bio needs a **lead line** of your own, for example: “The untold stories behind the billion dollar companies”. Don't copy the examples.
- A new account or a rebranded one from an old Atomik campaign are both fine. Rebranded accounts must be fully archived and set up again.

Non-campaign clips are **not** about the campaign. They don't follow the campaign rules; they just fit the same **business / tech / startups / growth** niche and look like normal, organic posts. This keeps your page natural and gets your campaign clips more reach.

For every **3 campaign clips**, post **1 non-campaign clip**. This is required for your submissions to be accepted. Non-campaign clips must contain **no Distribution footage, logo, name, or any reference to the show, its hosts, or its guests**.

## Extra requirements from the complete campaign archive

The user-provided combined archive adds these important restrictions. Treat them as hard rules for this Distribution campaign:

- Do not post, schedule, automate comments, or use an uploader without explicit user approval. Automated comments require a prior moderation-team ticket and can cause posts to be taken down. Never use view bots or fake engagement.
